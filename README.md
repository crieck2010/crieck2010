## 🚀 Flagship Ecosystems

### 1. [SurveySuite](https://github.com/crieck2010/survey-suite) — Field-to-Finish Land Surveying
A comprehensive computational suite taking land surveyors from raw field data to legally defensible PDF plats:
* **[survey-drafting](https://github.com/crieck2010/survey-drafting)**: Pure-Python vector PDF 1.4 drafting engine with a validity gate and automated metes-and-bounds legal descriptions.
* **[survey-adjust-workflow](https://github.com/crieck2010/survey-adjust-workflow)**: User-weighted least-squares adjustment, Baarda data snooping, and formal justification reports.
* **[survey-crs](https://github.com/crieck2010/survey-crs)**: Pure-Python registry of 345 EPSG-researched coordinate reference systems (SPCS, UTM, Lambert, Transverse Mercator).
* **[survey-cogo](https://github.com/crieck2010/survey-cogo)**: Inverses, intersections, traverses, and area computations with zero external dependencies.

### 2. [NCEES Exam Prep](https://github.com/crieck2010/ncees-exam-prep) — Mobile & Algorithmic Learning
A cross-platform mobile ecosystem and parameterized question generator for engineering and surveying licensure:
* **Generative Question Engine**: 48 deterministic math and theory generators across all 7 NCEES FS knowledge areas.
* **Mobile Quiz Client (Flutter)**: SM-2 spaced repetition with confidence grading, Question of the Day (QOTD), streaks, and exam countdowns.
* **Expanding Family**: Foundations for upcoming training apps for **NCEES PS**, **FE Civil**, **PE**, and **State-Specific PLS** exams.

### 3. [TradeSuite & Agentic Framework](https://github.com/crieck2010/trade-agents) — Autonomous Multi-Agent Desks
Institutional-grade quantitative architectures and research tooling:
* **Multi-Agent Research Desk**: Adversarial debate, data auditing (survivorship and split discontinuities), and research intake ledgers.
* **Occam's Razor Ablations**: Complexity-budget enforcement and out-of-sample backtest validation.

### 4. [EarthWatch & Geospatial Media](https://github.com/crieck2010/earthwatch-suite) — Satellite & Ocean Flow
* Automated vertical MP4 reel rendering from NOAA OFS surface currents, sea surface temperatures, and satellite site monitoring.
